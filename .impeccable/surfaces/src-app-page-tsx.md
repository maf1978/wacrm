# WACRM Landing

- Scope: `/`, public marketing landing page.
- Mode: Persuade.
- Audience: companies evaluating an installable conversational CRM.
- Job: understand how WACRM turns an incoming conversation into coordinated action.
- Primary action: request a demonstration.
- Proof: truthful product capabilities demonstrated with synthetic examples explicitly labeled as demonstrations.
- Constraints: Spanish-first, visible login access, no fabricated customers, testimonials, commercial metrics, or outcomes.
- Direction: Centro de rutas conversacionales.
- Memorable moment: one vertical signal spine carries the visitor through Mensaje, Decide, Actúa, and Crece.
- Approved composition: option C, vertical signal spine.
- Unresolved: final destination and handling for demo requests beyond the existing signup path.
